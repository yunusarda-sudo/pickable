# Pickable — is your site easy to discover?

Pickable analyzes public websites for AI-agent readiness signals and basic marketing visibility. It returns asynchronous scan status, redacted normalized findings and a preliminary score.

- Full agent guide: https://solveny.dev/llms-full.txt

Scan history, statuses, reports, previews, and intelligence reads are scoped to the caller’s direct peer IP address. Forwarded-IP headers are not trusted; clients behind a reverse proxy may share the same scope. This is not account authentication or a confidentiality guarantee. Provider evidence is redacted and raw-source endpoints return 404. Submit public targets only; never include credentials or secrets.

Reports combine external Ora and Cloudflare observations with a small marketing playbook. Scores describe measured observations, not proof of successful agent tasks or a promise of referrals, rankings, or revenue.
