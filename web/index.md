# Pickable — is your site easy to discover?

Pickable analyzes public websites for AI-agent readiness signals and basic marketing visibility. It returns asynchronous scan status, normalized findings, source evidence, and a preliminary score.

- Full agent guide: https://solveny.dev/llms-full.txt
- Source repository: https://github.com/ozgureyilmaz/pickable

The free public demo scans public domain names. Scan history, status, reports, raw source results, and preview metadata are publicly readable. Do not submit private URLs, credentials, or secrets.

Reports combine external Ora and Cloudflare observations with a small marketing playbook. Scores describe measured observations, not proof of successful agent tasks or a promise of referrals, rankings, or revenue.
