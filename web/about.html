About the product

Make your website easier
to understand.

Pickable gives site owners a concise view of public signals that can affect discovery and use by people, search systems, and AI agents.

What Pickable checks

A scan combines findings from Ora and Cloudflare's isitagentready.com with a small marketing playbook that looks at linked social profiles and blog activity. Reports show normalized findings and which checks were not measured; provider payloads stay on the server.

Agent readiness

Discovery, content, and integration observations reported by external sources.

Marketing basics

Simple observations about social presence and recent publishing.

Evidence first

Normalized findings preserve source attribution and measurement coverage.

What a score means

Scores summarize available observations under the report's scoring rules. They do not establish that an independent agent can finish a task, bring referrals, improve rankings, or generate sales. Source coverage and scoring methods can change.

Who it is for

Website owners, indie builders, SaaS teams, and developers who want to inspect how their public site appears to automated discovery systems.

Use a public domain. Do not submit credentials or private URLs. Scan history, reports, and previews are scoped to the caller’s direct network peer IP. This is not account authentication: callers behind the same proxy may share access. Raw provider responses are not exposed. Submit only public URLs without credentials.

Pickable is a public demo. Read the documentation, API guide, or demo and limits.
