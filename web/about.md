# About Pickable

Pickable gives website owners a concise view of public signals that can affect discovery and use by people, search systems, and AI agents.

A scan combines findings from Ora and Cloudflare's isitagentready.com with a small marketing playbook that looks at linked social profiles and blog activity. Reports redact provider evidence and indicate checks that were not measured.

Scores summarize available observations under the report's scoring rules. They do not establish that an independent agent can finish a task, bring referrals, improve rankings, or generate sales. Source coverage and scoring methods can change.

Pickable is for website owners, indie builders, SaaS teams, and developers who want to inspect how a public site appears to automated discovery systems.

Scan history, statuses, reports, previews, and intelligence reads are scoped to the caller’s direct peer IP address. Forwarded-IP headers are not trusted; clients behind a reverse proxy may share the same scope. This is not account authentication or a confidentiality guarantee. Provider evidence is redacted and raw-source endpoints return 404. Use public targets only; never submit credentials or secrets.
